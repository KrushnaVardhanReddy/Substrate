⚠️ CRITICAL CONTEXT: GQLPARSER FEDERATION INJECTION ⚠️
`vektah/gqlparser` natively fails if it encounters `@key` without a directive definition. You must programmatically inject the Apollo Federation directives (`directive @key(fields: _FieldSet!) repeatable on OBJECT | INTERFACE`, etc.) into the schema source *before* running `gqlparser.LoadSchema` if you detect they are being used but not explicitly defined.
