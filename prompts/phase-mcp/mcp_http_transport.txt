The main multiplexer is initialized in `api/cmd/server/main.go`.

⚠️ CRITICAL CONTEXT: SSE TRANSPORT SPEC ⚠️
The client first GETs `/mcp/sse`. You must respond with `Content-Type: text/event-stream`, keep the connection open, and immediately send an `event: endpoint\ndata: /mcp/messages?sessionId=<uuid>\n\n`. 
The client will then POST JSON-RPC messages to `/mcp/messages?sessionId=<uuid>`. You must parse the JSON-RPC request, pass it to `mcpServer.HandleMessage`, and then broadcast the resulting JSON-RPC response back over the SSE channel mapped to that `sessionId`.
